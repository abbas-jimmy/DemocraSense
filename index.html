01 — Foundation

What is Voting?

Voting is the act of expressing your preference or choice in an election. It is the cornerstone of democracy — the system where the people hold the power to decide who governs them and what policies shape their lives.

When you vote, you are participating in a collective decision-making process enshrined in the Constitution of India. Your vote is your voice in government — it is constitutionally protected and legally guaranteed under Article 326.

Elections happen at local, state, and national levels — from your Gram Panchayat to the Lok Sabha. Each election shapes laws, budgets, schools, healthcare, and your daily life.

🏛

Lok Sabha Elections

Lower house of Parliament — 543 constituencies elect Members of Parliament (MPs)

🏙

State Vidhan Sabha

State legislative assemblies — elect MLAs who form state governments

🏘

Panchayat & Municipal

Local body elections — Gram Panchayat, Municipal Corporations, Ward Councillors

📋

Rajya Sabha

Upper house members elected by State Legislative Assemblies, not directly by voters

Indian Voter Requirements

  • ✓
    Age 18+ — Must be at least 18 years old on the qualifying date (Jan 1 of election year)
  • ✓
    Indian Citizen — Must be a citizen of India (by birth or naturalisation)
  • ✓
    Ordinary Resident — Must be an ordinary resident of the constituency where you register
  • ✓
    On Electoral Roll — Must be enrolled on the electoral roll (voter list) of your constituency
  • ✗
    Not of Unsound Mind as declared by a competent court
  • ✗
    Not Disqualified under any law relating to corrupt practices or election offences
📌 Note: The Election Commission of India manages all elections. You can register or check your voter status on the voters.eci.gov.in portal using your Aadhaar or other ID proof.
03 — The Process

How Elections Work

A step-by-step visual guide to the entire election cycle.

1

Election Commission Announces Schedule

The Election Commission of India (ECI) announces the election schedule — including polling dates, nomination deadlines, and the Model Code of Conduct (MCC) comes into force immediately.

~4–8 weeks before Polling Day
2

Nominations Filed

Candidates file nomination papers with the Returning Officer. Independent and party candidates submit their affidavits disclosing assets, liabilities, and criminal records.

Nomination period
3

Scrutiny & Withdrawal

Nomination papers are scrutinized. Candidates may withdraw within a set period. The final list of candidates is published and symbol allotment takes place.

After nomination deadline
4

Voter Roll Verification

Ensure your name is on the electoral roll at voters.eci.gov.in. Voter ID (EPIC card) is the primary ID at polling booths. New registrations must be completed before rolls are finalized.

Before rolls are finalized
5

Campaign Period

Parties and candidates campaign within ECI guidelines. All campaign spending is monitored. Campaigning must stop 48 hours before polling (silence period).

Until 48 hrs before polling
6

🗳 Polling Day!

India uses Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs). Voters queue at their assigned booth, verify identity, press the button next to their chosen candidate, and receive an ink mark on their finger as proof.

Announced date(s) by ECI
7

Vote Counting & Results

EVM data is counted on a separate date under strict security. Results are declared constituency-by-constituency. VVPAT slips provide a paper audit trail for verification.

Counting day (announced by ECI)
8

Government Formation

The winning party or coalition stakes claim to form the government. The President (national) or Governor (state) invites the leader to be sworn in as Prime Minister or Chief Minister.

After results & coalition talks
04 — How to Vote

Step-by-Step: Casting Your Vote

Step 1
📝

Register on Electoral Roll

Visit voters.eci.gov.in or your nearest BLO (Booth Level Officer). Fill Form 6 for new registration. You'll receive your EPIC (Voter ID) card.

Step 2
🗺

Find Your Polling Booth

Use the Voter Helpline App or voters.eci.gov.in to find your assigned booth. Your booth number is also printed on your Voter ID slip.

Step 3
🪪

Bring Valid ID

EPIC (Voter ID) is primary. Alternatives accepted: Aadhaar, Passport, Driving Licence, PAN Card, MNREGA Job Card, or passbook with photo.

Step 4
📋

Check Voter Slip

Polling agents distribute voter slips before election day with your serial number. This speeds up your turn at the booth — carry it along with your ID.

Step 5
🗳

Go to the Polling Booth

Arrive during voting hours (typically 7 AM – 6 PM). Join the queue. Presiding Officers manage orderly voting. If in line before closing, you have the right to vote.

Step 6
☑️

Press the EVM Button

After identity verification, press the blue button on the EVM next to your candidate's name and symbol. The VVPAT machine will briefly show your choice. Your left index finger gets inked.

Ways to Vote

🏫 EVM Voting (Polling Day)

Visit your assigned booth on polling day and vote using the Electronic Voting Machine — the standard method across India.

📬 Postal Ballot

Available to service voters (armed forces, police on duty), senior citizens (80+), persons with disabilities, and COVID-affected voters.

🏠 Home Voting (PwD/Senior)

ECI introduced facility-based voting for persons with disabilities (40%+ disability) and voters aged 85+ to vote from home with a mobile team.

🌐 NRI Voting

Non-Resident Indians registered on electoral rolls can vote in person at their constituency. Online/proxy voting is not yet widely available.

05 — Choosing Candidates

How to Decide Who to Vote For

Evaluating candidates wisely is a skill. Here's a framework to help you make an informed choice.

🔍

Research Their Record

Look at what they've actually done, not just what they say. Past votes, past statements, and past actions are more reliable than campaign promises.

  • Check voting records on PRS Legislative Research (prsindia.org)
  • Read their affidavit on affidavit.eci.gov.in
  • Look at endorsements and campaign donors (ADR India)
📰

Use Multiple News Sources

Read from diverse, reputable sources. Cross-check claims. No single outlet has the full picture.

  • Check fact-checkers: Alt News, Factly, Boom Live
  • Read across the political spectrum
  • Distinguish news from opinion/editorial
⚖️

Compare on Issues That Matter to You

What issues affect your life? Education, healthcare, housing, economy? Find out where each candidate stands.

  • Use MyNeta.info to compare candidate backgrounds
  • Watch debates with an open mind
  • Consider both short and long-term impacts
🚩

Watch for Red Flags

Certain behaviors signal a candidate may not be trustworthy or effective in office.

  • Vague or contradictory promises
  • Appeals to fear without solutions
  • Refusal to engage with evidence
  • History of corruption or ethical violations
🤝

Consider Their Character

Leadership qualities matter. Look for honesty, accountability, and respect for democratic institutions and the rule of law.

  • Do they accept responsibility for mistakes?
  • Do they respect the opposition?
  • Do they uphold constitutional norms?
🧠

Think Independently

Your vote is private and personal. Don't let peer pressure or social media dictate your choice. Make an informed, independent decision.

  • Verify before you share
  • Be skeptical of viral claims
  • Your values are your own
06 — Rules & Regulations

Voting Rules You Must Know

🔒 Your Rights at the Booth

  • You have the right to vote free from intimidation or undue influence
  • You can request assistance if you are a person with disability
  • If in queue when polls close, you may still vote
  • You have the right to secrecy of your vote (Representation of People Act)
  • You have the right to NOTA (None of the Above) as an option

🚫 What's NOT Allowed

  • Voting more than once (personation) — criminal offence
  • Bribing voters with cash, gifts, or liquor (Model Code of Conduct)
  • Campaigning within 100 metres of a polling booth on polling day
  • Carrying weapons near polling stations
  • Using government vehicles for campaigning during MCC period
  • Fake news/deepfakes to influence voters

📅 Key Deadlines

  • New voter registration (Form 6): Before electoral roll is finalised
  • Address change (Form 8A): Before roll revision deadline
  • Name correction (Form 8): Anytime via BLO or online
  • Qualifying date for age 18: January 1 of the reference year

🆔 ID Documents Accepted

  • Primary: EPIC (Voter ID card)
  • Alternatives: Aadhaar, Passport, Driving Licence
  • Also accepted: PAN Card, MNREGA Job Card, Bank passbook with photo
  • Check your booth slip from the ECI before polling day

⚖️ Election Commission Powers

  • ECI is an independent constitutional body (Article 324)
  • Can repoll at any booth if malpractice is detected
  • Can remove officials obstructing elections
  • Model Code of Conduct binding on all parties and candidates
  • EVMs and VVPATs used nationwide for tamper-proof voting

📣 Reporting Problems

  • Call ECI Voter Helpline: 1950
  • Use the cVIGIL app to report MCC violations with photo/video
  • Contact your Returning Officer or Presiding Officer
  • File a complaint at eci.gov.in
